Cross-Border Payments

BridgePay — Remittance Platform Modernisation

Problem Statement

The existing remittance platform relied heavily on manual processing steps, leading to high operational overhead, frequent compliance gaps, and significant transaction delays that impacted customer satisfaction and regulatory standing.

My Role & Contributions

Acted as the lead Business Analyst responsible for end-to-end requirement gathering across business, compliance, and technology stakeholders. Produced comprehensive BRD documentation, authored user stories with clear acceptance criteria, and facilitated cross-functional workshops to align teams on the target state solution.

Approach

Conducted As-Is process mapping to identify bottlenecks and manual touchpoints. Collaborated with compliance teams to incorporate regulatory requirements (AML/KYC) into the To-Be process design. Defined precise acceptance criteria and coordinated UAT cycles to validate that the implemented solution met business expectations.

✦ Outcome: Streamlined transaction flow with reduced manual processing effort, improved compliance traceability, and faster end-to-end remittance cycle times.
Risk & Compliance

Intelligent Fraud Detection System

Problem Statement

The organisation's legacy transaction monitoring process was slow to flag suspicious activity, creating a widening window of financial exposure. Fraud cases were often identified days after occurrence, resulting in significant monetary and reputational risk.

My Role & Contributions

Responsible for preparing detailed BRD and FRD artefacts, defining business rules for fraud scoring and alert thresholds, and designing comprehensive UAT scenarios. Worked closely with data science and risk teams to translate detection model logic into testable business requirements.

Approach

Facilitated requirement workshops with fraud analysts to capture detection logic and edge cases. Defined rule-based triggers and exception workflows in the FRD. Built a UAT test matrix covering positive/negative transaction scenarios, and coordinated user sign-off with the risk operations team.

✦ Outcome: Significantly faster fraud identification, reduced financial exposure, and a scalable detection framework that improved the organisation's overall risk posture.
Operations

Smart ATM Cash Management

Problem Statement

Recurring ATM cash-out events were damaging customer trust and increasing operational costs due to reactive, manual replenishment processes with no demand forecasting capability. Branch operations teams lacked visibility into real-time cash levels across the ATM network.

My Role & Contributions

Led requirements gathering for the predictive cash management module, designed process flows for the automated replenishment workflow, and authored user stories for the operations and technology teams. Coordinated with the ATM vendor and internal ops stakeholders to define integration requirements.

Approach

Mapped the current-state replenishment process to identify failure points and data gaps. Defined automation triggers, alert thresholds, and escalation workflows in the To-Be design. Worked with the data team to specify historical transaction data requirements for the forecasting model.

✦ Outcome: Reduced ATM cash-out incidents through predictive restocking, lower manual intervention effort for operations teams, and improved customer availability across the ATM network.
Insurance Analytics

PawSure Insurance KPI Analytics

Problem Statement

KPI tracking across PawSure's insurance portfolios was conducted entirely through manual spreadsheet compilation, creating reporting bottlenecks, data inconsistencies, and significant delays in executive-level decision-making.

My Role & Contributions

Served as the Business Analyst responsible for SQL-driven data analysis, data model documentation, and translating analytical findings into stakeholder-ready reporting insights. Collaborated with the product and executive teams to define the KPI framework and reporting cadence.

Approach

Developed a suite of SQL queries to extract, cleanse, and aggregate policy and claims data from multiple source systems. Built data models to support recurring KPI dashboards and worked with the BI team to translate outputs into visual reporting formats. Validated data accuracy against legacy manual reports during transition.

✦ Outcome: Eliminated manual reporting effort, established a reliable single source of truth for insurance KPIs, and enabled faster, more confident data-driven decisions at the executive level.
